# Licensing status

No blanket licence has yet been selected for all Spa-Bench artifacts. Until a
licence is added, the original project material remains protected by default
copyright rules.

LeRobot-derived patches retain their upstream copyright headers and are subject
to the Apache License 2.0 distributed with LeRobot. Model weights and datasets
may have additional upstream or platform-specific terms; their cards must be
checked before redistribution.

Before public release, select explicit licences for:

- original source code;
- benchmark manifests and tabular annotations;
- figures and written documentation;
- the thesis/report and presentation.
